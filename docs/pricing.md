# Pricing evaluation (October 2026)

Why BetriX costs what it does, and what has to stay true for it to make money.
Prices live in `src/lib/pricing.ts`; this is the reasoning behind them.

Assumptions: ₦1,550 to the dollar (re-run the numbers if the rate moves far);
Paystack local cards at 1.5% + ₦100 (the ₦100 is waived under ₦2,500, the fee
is capped at ₦2,000).

## What it costs to run

### Fixed, per month (once the site takes payments)

| Item | $ | ₦ |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel Pro (the free Hobby plan doesn't allow commercial use) | 20 | 31,000 |
| Supabase Pro (the free tier pauses and caps the database) | 25 | 38,750 |
| TheSportsDB premium (the only paid sports feed) | 9 | 13,950 |
| Resend (free up to 3,000 emails a month; Pro is $20 after) | 0-20 | 0-31,000 |
| Domain (.com.ng) | ~1 | ~1,500 |
| **Total** | **~55-75** | **~₦85,000-116,000** |

Everything else (GitHub Actions on a public repo, Supabase cron and edge
functions, The Odds API free tier) is inside free limits; see
`ingestion/README.md`.

### Variable, per use

| What | Model / service | Cost each |
|---|---|---|
| One Ask BetriX question (2-3 tool rounds, cached system prompt) | Claude Haiku 5.5, $0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens (was Sonnet 5.5, $2 / $10) | ~$0.001-0.002, **~₦1.5-3** (was ~₦25-50) |
| One written match analysis | Claude Sonnet 5.5, $2 / $10 per million tokens (was Opus 5, $5 / $25) | ~$0.012, **~₦18** (was ~₦45) |
| SportyBet price lookups | parse.bot, metered | check the parse.bot dashboard |

The match analysis used to be regenerated by every server instance every hour.
It is now stored once per match and model read (`match_analyses`, migration
0035), so it costs roughly one generation per game rather than dozens.

## Margin per plan

Worked at ₦60 a question, the Opus 5.5 cost these prices were set against.
Ask BetriX now runs on Haiku 5.5 at ~₦2-3 a question, so the AI figures
below are close to zero and the margins are roughly price minus Paystack. "Heavy" is a user at the daily cap.

| Plan | Price | Paystack | AI, typical | Margin, typical | AI at the cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day pass | ₦500 | ₦8 | 5 questions, ₦300 | **₦190** | 30 questions, ₦1,800 |
| Weekend pass | ₦1,000 | ₦15 | 10 questions, ₦600 | **₦385** | — |
| Week pass | ₦2,000 | ₦30 | 15 questions, ₦900 | **₦1,070** | — |
| Pro, monthly | ₦5,000 | ₦175 | 1 a day, ₦1,800 | **₦3,025** | 30 a day, ₦54,000 |
| VIP, monthly | ₦12,000 | ₦280 | 3 a day, ₦5,400 | **₦6,320** | 100 a day, ₦180,000 |

At the old prices a Day pass (₦250) lost money on an ordinary user and Pro
(₦3,500) kept about ₦1,550 a month. Break-even on fixed costs now needs about
**30 Pro-equivalent subscribers** (₦85,000 ÷ ~₦3,000), down from about 55.

## What changed

- **Passes**: Day ₦250 → **₦500**, Weekend ₦700 → **₦1,000**, Week ₦1,200 →
  **₦2,000**. Four Week passes (₦8,000) still cost more than a month of Pro.
- **Pro**: ₦3,500 → **₦5,000** a month; quarterly ₦13,500 (10% off), yearly
  ₦48,000 (20% off).
- **VIP**: ₦8,000 → **₦12,000** a month; yearly ₦115,200 (20% off).
- **Ask BetriX allowances**: free accounts 5 → **3** questions a day; Pass and
  Pro 150 → **30** a day; VIP **100** a day. Paid plans are still presented as
  unlimited, fair use.
- Forge, predictions, live scores and every stats page stay free: they cost
  nothing per view, and they are what brings people in from search.

## Still to decide

1. **Update the Paystack plans.** Subscriptions are charged the amount set on
   each Paystack Plan, not the amount in the code. Edit the Pro and VIP plan
   amounts in Paystack (choosing whether existing subscribers move to the new
   price), or create new plans and list the old codes in
   `PAYSTACK_LEGACY_PLAN_CODES` to keep current members on their old price.
   Passes are charged from the code and change on deploy.
2. **Measure the real AI cost.** The per-question figure is an estimate. Two
   weeks of the Anthropic console's daily spend divided by questions asked
   replaces it.
3. **Model choice for Ask BetriX.** Switched to Claude Haiku 5.5 (October
   2026), about a twentieth of Sonnet 5.5 per question. If answers on harder
   questions (accas, price comparisons) fall short, Sonnet 5.5 is the step up.
4. **VAT.** Once registered, 7.5% VAT applies to digital services; decide
   whether these prices include it.
5. **Vercel Pro** before taking payments in volume (Hobby terms).
